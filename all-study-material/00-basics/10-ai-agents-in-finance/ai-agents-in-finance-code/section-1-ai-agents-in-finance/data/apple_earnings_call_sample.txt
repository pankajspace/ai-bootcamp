Apple Inc. - Fiscal 2025 Third Quarter Earnings Call (illustrative)

Operator: Good afternoon and welcome to Apple's fiscal third quarter conference call.

CEO: Thank you. This quarter we delivered revenue of $98.7 billion, up 6 percent year over year,
a June-quarter record. iPhone revenue grew on strong demand for our latest models, Mac grew double
digits helped by new laptops, and iPad returned to growth. Services set an all-time revenue record,
and our installed base of active devices reached a new high in every geographic segment.

CFO: Diluted earnings per share were $1.72, up 9 percent year over year. Gross margin was
46.5 percent, up 60 basis points, driven by a favourable Services mix. We returned over $25 billion
to shareholders through dividends and share buybacks during the quarter.

CFO: For the September quarter we expect total revenue to grow in the low to mid single digits year
over year, gross margin between 46 and 47 percent, and operating expenses between $15.6 and
$15.8 billion.

Analyst Q&A highlights:
- Asked about China, management noted softer demand and intense local competition.
- Asked about regulation, management acknowledged ongoing App Store and antitrust proceedings in the
  US and EU that could affect Services economics.
- Asked about supply, management flagged possible component cost pressure and tariff uncertainty.

CEO: We remain confident in our product roadmap and in the long-term opportunity ahead.
