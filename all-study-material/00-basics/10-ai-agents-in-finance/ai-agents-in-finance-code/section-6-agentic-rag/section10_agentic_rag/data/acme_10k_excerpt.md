# Acme Robotics, Inc. — Form 10-K (excerpt), fiscal year ended December 31, 2024

## Item 7. Management's discussion — results of operations

Total revenue for fiscal 2024 was $612.4 million, up 14% from $537.2 million in fiscal 2023. Software and services revenue grew 31% to $198.6 million and now represents 32% of total revenue.

Gross margin was 43.1% in fiscal 2024, up from 40.2% in fiscal 2023. The expansion was driven mainly by the shift in revenue mix toward higher-margin software subscriptions, partly offset by higher component costs for the warehouse-robot line.

Net income was $96.3 million. Diluted earnings per share were $1.92, compared with $1.51 in fiscal 2023. EBITDA was $131.0 million.

## Liquidity and capital resources

At December 31, 2024, current assets were $498.0 million and current liabilities were $307.4 million, giving a current ratio of 1.62. Excluding inventories of $119.8 million, the quick ratio was 1.23.

Cash and cash equivalents were $142.5 million. Total long-term debt was $180.0 million, all of it the term loan under our credit facility. Net debt to EBITDA was 0.29x.

## Credit facility covenants

Our $250 million revolving credit and term loan facility requires us to maintain (i) a minimum current ratio of 1.25 and (ii) a maximum net leverage ratio (net debt to EBITDA) of 3.0x, both tested quarterly. We were in compliance with all covenants at December 31, 2024.

## Item 1A. Risk factors (selected)

Customer concentration: our largest customer, a global e-commerce retailer, accounted for 41% of fiscal 2024 revenue. The loss of this customer would materially reduce our revenue and profitability.

Supply chain: we depend on a single supplier for the actuators used in our humanoid robot line.
