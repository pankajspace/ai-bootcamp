# Acme Robotics — Q3 fiscal 2025 earnings call (transcript excerpt), October 28, 2025

**Operator:** Welcome to Acme Robotics' third-quarter fiscal 2025 earnings call.

**Maria Lopez, Chief Financial Officer:** Thank you. Q3 revenue was $171.8 million, up 16% year over year, with software subscriptions again the fastest-growing line. Gross margin for the quarter was 43.8%.

On the balance sheet: our quick ratio at quarter-end, September 30, 2025, was 1.18, down from 1.23 at year-end. The decline is deliberate — we built inventory ahead of the humanoid launch. The current ratio was 1.58.

**David Kim, Chief Executive Officer:** Demand is strong. We are raising full-year fiscal 2025 revenue guidance to between $690 million and $705 million.

**Sam Ortiz, sell-side analyst:** Should investors worry about liquidity with inventory this high?

**Maria Lopez:** No. We remain well above the covenant minimum current ratio of 1.25 in our credit facility, and we expect the quick ratio to recover in Q1 as the inventory converts to sales.

**Priya Nair, buy-side analyst:** Is customer concentration changing?

**David Kim:** Our largest customer is still a little above 40% of revenue, but the new humanoid contracts should dilute that over the next year.
