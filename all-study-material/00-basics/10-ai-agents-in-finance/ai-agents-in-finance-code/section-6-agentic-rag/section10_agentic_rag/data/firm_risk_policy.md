# Future with Shivank Bank — Investment Liquidity and Counterparty Risk Policy (v3.2, effective January 1, 2025)

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every new long equity position opened by the bank's managed funds.

## 2. Issuer liquidity requirements for a new long position

2.1 Quick ratio: the issuer's quick ratio must be at least 1.00.

2.2 Current ratio: a current ratio of at least 1.50 is standard. Between 1.25 and 1.50, a Risk Committee exception is required. Below 1.25, new positions are prohibited.

2.3 Leverage: the issuer's net debt to EBITDA must not exceed 3.0x.

2.4 Source of figures: the issuer's most recent audited annual report (Form 10-K) is the controlling source. More recent quarterly figures (10-Q, earnings calls) must be reviewed for deterioration and noted in the investment memo. If a quarterly figure breaches a limit in this section, escalate to the Risk Committee before trading.

## 3. Concentration

3.1 Exposure to a single issuer must not exceed 5% of the fund's net asset value.

3.2 Issuers whose largest customer exceeds 40% of revenue require enhanced due diligence before purchase.

## 4. Covenant awareness

Analysts must record the issuer's financial covenants and the current headroom against each one in the investment memo.
