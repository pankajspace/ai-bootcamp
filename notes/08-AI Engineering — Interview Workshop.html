An assistant for company policies

01

You have built an assistant that answers employee questions about leave, expenses and equipment. The company has 200 policy documents, and some change every week. In the interview, explain how you would take the small handbook prototype and turn it into a reliable service for employees.

Discuss each follow-up.
Reveal hints and answers when ready.

Project requirements

  • Answers must use the policy that applies to the employee and include a source.
  • The first release answers questions; it does not update employee records.
  • The prototype can run locally, but the deployed service cannot depend on your laptop.
  • The team wants managed agent hosting and has no private-network dependency for the first release.
