Words you will see

Chunk: a small piece of a bigger document. Overlap: a few characters repeated at the start of the next chunk, so a sentence cut in half still appears whole somewhere. Vector store: a database that saves chunks with their embeddings and finds the closest ones to a question.

2.5 HyDE: search with a fake answer

Story: A shopper types "I want running shoes". The product titles say trainers, sneakers, Ultraboost, Pegasus... almost no words match. Keyword search (BM25) finds only one product. HyDE fixes this: ask an LLM to imagine what matching products would be called, and search with that imagined text.

Need a hint?

  • np.argsort(scores)[::-1][:3] gives the indexes of the 3 best products
You are done when
  • "WITHOUT HyDE" shows only 1 product
  • "WITH HyDE" shows 3 running products (for example Pegasus, Ultraboost, Fresh Foam, Gel-Kayano) with much higher scores

Think about it

  1. Why did we add 10% overlap between chunks in 2.1? What could go wrong with zero overlap?
  2. You are building a RAG bot over a 300-page HR policy PDF with clear section headings. Which strategy would you start with, and why?
  3. Semantic chunking needs an embedding call for every sentence. When is that cost worth it?
  4. HyDE's fake answer may contain wrong facts (for example a shoe model that does not exist). Why does that not break the search?
  5. In 2.4, how could the Metadata (headings) help when a user asks "What is clustering?"
