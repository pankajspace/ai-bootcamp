# Employee Leave Policy

Applies to all full-time employees of Nimbu Mart in India. Owner: People Team.

## Leave types and yearly entitlement

- **Earned leave (EL):** 18 days per calendar year, credited at 1.5 days per month. Unused earned leave can be carried forward, up to a maximum balance of 30 days.
- **Casual leave (CL):** 12 days per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward and cannot be encashed.
- **Sick leave (SL):** 12 days per year. A doctor's certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.
- **Maternity leave:** 26 weeks of paid leave, as per the Maternity Benefit Act.
- **Paternity leave:** 10 working days, to be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.
- **Bereavement leave:** 5 working days for the loss of an immediate family member.

## Public holidays

The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The list is published every December on the People portal.

## Work from home

Employees in corporate roles may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Warehouse and delivery roles are not eligible.

## Applying for leave

Apply on the People portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Leave is approved by your reporting manager.
