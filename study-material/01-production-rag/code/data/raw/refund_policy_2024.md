# Nimbu Mart Returns Policy (2024) - ARCHIVED

Status: archived. This policy applied to orders delivered between 1 January 2024 and 31 March 2025. It has been replaced by the 2025 Returns and Refund Policy.

## Return windows

- **Electronics:** 10 days from delivery.
- **Fashion and footwear:** 30 days from delivery.
- **Groceries:** Not returnable.

## Pickup fee

A pickup fee of ₹29 applied to change-of-mind returns for all customers.

## Refund timelines

Card refunds took 7 to 10 business days. UPI refunds took 3 to 5 business days.
