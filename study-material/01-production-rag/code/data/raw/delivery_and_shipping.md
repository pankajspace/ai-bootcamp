# Delivery and Shipping at Nimbu Mart

## Delivery speed by city tier

Nimbu Mart groups pincodes into three tiers.

- **Tier-1 metros** (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Pune): standard delivery in 2 days.
- **Tier-2 cities** (for example Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore, Coimbatore, Kochi): standard delivery in 3 to 4 days.
- **Tier-3 towns and rural pincodes:** standard delivery in 5 to 7 days.

## Nimbu Express (same-day delivery)

Nimbu Express is available only in Tier-1 metros. Orders placed before 12 noon are delivered the same day by 10 pm. Express costs ₹79 per order and is free for Nimbu Plus members.

## Delivery charges

Standard delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 have a delivery charge of ₹40.

## Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available on orders up to ₹5,000. COD is not available for gift cards, gold and silver coins, or orders shipped to Tier-3 rural pincodes during the monsoon season (June to September). You can pay the delivery partner by cash or by scanning their UPI QR code.

## Failed delivery attempts

The delivery partner makes up to 3 attempts. If all 3 attempts fail, the order is returned to origin (RTO) and cancelled. Prepaid orders that are returned to origin are refunded within 5 business days.

## Tracking statuses

Your order moves through these statuses: Confirmed, Packed, Shipped, Out for Delivery, Delivered. If the status has not changed for 72 hours, contact support from the order page.
