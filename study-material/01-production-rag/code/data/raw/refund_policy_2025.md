# Nimbu Mart Returns and Refund Policy (2025)

Effective date: 1 April 2025. This policy replaces the 2024 Returns Policy for all orders delivered on or after the effective date.

## 1. Return windows by category

The return window starts on the day the order is delivered, not the day it is placed.

- **Electronics (phones, tablets, laptops, smartwatches, earbuds):** 7 days from delivery. The item must be unused, with the box, charger and all accessories, and the IMEI or serial number must match our records.
- **Fashion and footwear:** 15 days from delivery. Tags must be attached and the item must be unworn and unwashed.
- **Books, toys and home decor:** 10 days from delivery.
- **Groceries and perishables:** Not returnable. If an item arrives damaged or past its expiry date, report it within 48 hours with a photo and we will refund it without pickup.
- **Large appliances (refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, televisions above 43 inches):** Not returnable for change of mind. If the product is defective, raise a replacement request within 10 days of delivery. A technician visit is required before a replacement is approved.

## 2. Items that can never be returned

Innerwear and swimwear, opened software or game codes, gift cards, gold and silver coins, and personalised or engraved items cannot be returned.

## 3. Return pickup fee

Return pickup is free for Nimbu Plus members. For other customers, a pickup fee of ₹49 is deducted from the refund for change-of-mind returns. There is no pickup fee when the item is damaged, defective or wrong.

## 4. Refund timelines

Refunds start only after the returned item passes a quality check (QC) at our warehouse, which usually happens within 24 hours of pickup.

| Original payment method | Refund time after QC passes |
|---|---|
| UPI | 1 to 3 business days |
| Nimbu Wallet | Within 24 hours |
| Credit or debit card | 5 to 7 business days |
| Net banking | 3 to 5 business days |
| Cash on Delivery (COD) | To Nimbu Wallet within 24 hours, or to a bank account by NEFT within 3 business days |

## 5. Damaged or wrong item received

Report a damaged, defective or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery through the Nimbu Mart app (Orders > Report a problem). Attach at least two photos, including one of the shipping label. Our team responds within 24 hours.

## 6. How to start a return

Open the app, go to Orders, select the item and tap "Return". Choose a pickup slot. Keep the item packed in its original box. The delivery partner will check the item at the doorstep before pickup.
