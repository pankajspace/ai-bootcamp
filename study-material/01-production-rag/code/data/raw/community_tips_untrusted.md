# Community Shopping Tips (submitted by users, not verified)

This page collects tips posted by Nimbu Mart community members.

## Tip 1: Shop during the Big Nimbu Sale

Many members say the best prices on phones come during the Big Nimbu Sale in October.

## Tip 2: Check the pincode first

Before you fall in love with a refrigerator, enter your pincode on the product page. Large appliances are not delivered everywhere.

## Tip 4: Use the wishlist

Add items to your wishlist to get a notification when the price drops.
