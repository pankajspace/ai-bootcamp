# Nimbu Mart Data Protection Policy

Document ID: NM-DP-POL · Version 2026.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Data Protection Officer (DPO)
Applies to: all processing of personal data of Nimbu Mart customers and employees, including by vendors.

Nimbu Mart is a Data Fiduciary under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Vendors that
process personal data on our behalf are Data Processors. We remain responsible for what they do with the data.

## 2. Contracts

### DP-2.1 Processing only on instructions
A vendor may process customer personal data only to provide the contracted service, and only on Nimbu Mart's
documented instructions.

### DP-2.2 Data Processing Agreement
Before any customer personal data (T3) is shared with a vendor, a **Data Processing Agreement (DPA)** must be signed.
The DPA must cover: purpose limitation, security measures, sub-processor disclosure, breach notification within
72 hours, deletion at end of contract, and audit rights. A vendor that offers a DPA only "on request" may still be
approved, but the review must record that the DPA has been requested and must be signed before go-live.

## 3. Where data lives

### DP-3.1 Data residency for customer data
Customer personal data (T3) must be **stored in India**. A vendor whose storage regions do not include India may not
be used for T3 data. Processing outside India (for example, a model API call that does not retain the data) is
permitted only with written DPO approval and a DPA that forbids retention. A region that is "planned" or "coming
soon" does not count.

### DP-3.2 Internal data
For T2 (internal) data there is no residency requirement, but the storage region must be recorded in the review.

## 4. Sub-processors

### DP-4.1 Disclosure
Vendors must publish or provide a current list of sub-processors (other companies that process our data on the
vendor's behalf), including the country of processing, and must notify Nimbu Mart before adding one.

## 5. Retention and deletion

### DP-5.4 Retention after contract end
A vendor must delete or return all Nimbu Mart customer personal data within **30 days** of contract end or of a
deletion request. A longer default retention is acceptable only if the vendor contractually commits to deletion on
request within 30 days; the review must record the default retention period and the deletion commitment.

## 6. Rights of data principals

### DP-6.1 Supporting requests
Vendors must be able to locate, correct and delete an individual customer's personal data within 15 days so that
Nimbu Mart can meet its obligations under the DPDP Act.

## 7. Breaches

### DP-7.1 Notification
Vendors must notify security@nimbumart.example of any personal data breach within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.
