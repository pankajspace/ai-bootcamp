# Nimbu Mart Vendor Security Questionnaire (template NM-SEC-Q)

Document ID: NM-SEC-Q · Version 2026.1 · Owner: Information Security
Send the relevant questions to the vendor when the review cannot verify a requirement from public material
(SEC-4.3). Record the vendor's answer, the name of the person who answered, and the date.

### Q-SEC-4.2 Independent assurance
Please share your most recent SOC 2 Type II report (with the audit period) or your ISO/IEC 27001 certificate
(with certificate number, issuing body and expiry date) covering the service we intend to use.

### Q-DP-3.1 Data residency
In which countries is customer content stored at rest for the plan we intend to buy? Can storage be pinned to India?
Is any content processed outside India, and if so, is it retained?

### Q-DP-4.1 Sub-processors
Please provide your current sub-processor list with the country of processing for each, and describe how you notify
customers of changes.

### Q-AI-2.1 Training on customer content
Do you use customer content (prompts, files, outputs) to train or improve models? If this is configurable, on which
plan is it disabled by contract, and how is the setting enforced?

### Q-AI-3.4 Model providers
Which third-party model providers receive our content? What do your agreements with them say about training and
retention?

### Q-DP-5.4 Retention and deletion
What is the default retention period for customer content after contract end? Do you commit to deletion within
30 days of a written request? How is deletion confirmed?

### Q-SEC-7.1 Single sign-on
Do you support SAML 2.0 or OIDC single sign-on? On which plan? Do you support SCIM de-provisioning?

### Q-SEC-9.3 Incident history
Have you had a security incident affecting customer data in the last 24 months? If so, please share the post-incident
report.

### Q-DP-2.2 Data Processing Agreement
Please share your standard DPA. Does it include terms for India's DPDP Act, 2023?
