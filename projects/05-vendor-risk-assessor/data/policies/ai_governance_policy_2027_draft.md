# Nimbu Mart AI Governance Policy (2027 Proposed Draft — NOT IN EFFECT)

Document ID: NM-AI-POL-2027 · Version 2027.0-draft · Status: DRAFT · Target Effective Date: 1 April 2027
Owner: Policy Working Group (Draft for internal comment only — do not use for current vendor approvals).

## 1. Proposed Cross-Border Reciprocity (Draft)

### DP-3.1-DRAFT Proposed Adequacy Corridor for 2027
Under this proposed 2027 draft, T3 customer personal data might be permitted in designated adequacy corridors
(such as European Union Frankfurt or Singapore) if a bilateral adequacy notification is issued by the Government of
India. **Warning: this draft clause is NOT active policy. Under active policy DP-3.1, all T3 customer personal data
must be stored in India.**

## 2. Proposed Automated Renewal (Draft)

### AI-5.2-DRAFT Streamlined Renewal for Low-Risk Tiers
For T1 and T2 tools with zero incidents over 24 months, the 2027 draft proposes automated annual renewal without a
manual security review. This proposal is not yet approved or active.
