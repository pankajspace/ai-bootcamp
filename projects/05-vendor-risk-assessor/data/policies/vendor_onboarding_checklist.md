# Nimbu Mart Vendor Onboarding Checklist (Procurement)

Document ID: NM-PROC-CHK · Version 2026.1 · Effective 1 January 2026 · Owner: Head of Procurement
Applies to: every new SaaS or AI tool, and every change of data tier for an existing tool.

## 1. Approval status

### PROC-1.1 Approved Vendor List
A tool may be used only if it appears on the **Approved Vendor List** (NM-PROC-AVL) with an approved data tier that
is **equal to or higher than** the tier of data the team wants to use it with. A vendor approved for T2 (internal)
data is **not** approved for T3 (customer data) until a new review is recorded. The Approved Vendor List is
maintained by Procurement and is visible to the Procurement and Information Security groups.

### PROC-1.2 Requesting a review
Teams request a review by opening a ticket in the Procurement queue with: tool name, vendor website, business
purpose, data tier, number of users, and the plan they intend to buy.

## 2. The review file

### PROC-2.1 What the review file must contain
1. Vendor identity: legal entity name, country, website. Confirm it is the same company as the one on the website
   you assessed (several vendors share names).
2. The vendor risk assessment: every policy requirement with PASS / FAIL / UNKNOWN / NEEDS REVIEW, each with the
   source URL and the date the source was checked.
3. Open questions sent to the vendor (template NM-SEC-Q) and their answers.
4. The signed DPA (for T3).
5. The approver's decision and date.

### PROC-2.2 Evidence dates
Evidence older than 15 months is treated as stale and must be re-checked.

## 3. After approval

### PROC-3.1 Record
Procurement adds the vendor, the approved tier, the plan, the approval date and the next review date to the
Approved Vendor List.

### PROC-3.2 Changes
Any change in data tier, vendor ownership, storage region, or sub-processors triggers a new review (SEC-12.1).
